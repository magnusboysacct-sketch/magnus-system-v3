# Your Magnus account: plan, payment, support and feedback

This guide is for company directors and admins. It explains your plan, how to pay, how to let Magnus support help you, and how to send ideas or report a problem.

## Your plan

Open **My plan** in the menu. It shows:

- Your plan, and what is included
- When your free trial ends, or when the next bill is due
- Any bill to pay, with the payment details

💡 Only your company's director or admin can see the plan and pay the bill.

While Magnus is free (during the launch), My plan says "There is nothing to pay right now". If that changes, you will be told well before anything is charged.

### Changing plan

Contact Magnus support (WhatsApp or email, shown on the website and on My plan). You can change plan at any time.

## Paying a bill

1. Open **My plan** and look at the bill: the amount, the due date and the payment details.
2. Pay by **Lynk** or **bank transfer**, using the details shown.
3. Tap **I've paid**, and type the Lynk or bank reference number.
4. Magnus support checks the payment and confirms it. You get a notice when it is confirmed.

### Reminders

You get a reminder 7 days, 3 days and 1 day before a bill is due.

### If a bill is not paid

After a few grace days, your company account becomes **read-only**:

- Everyone can still sign in and see everything.
- Nobody can add or change anything until the bill is paid.
- Your data is safe, and nothing is deleted.

It unlocks the moment Magnus support confirms your payment.

⚠️ A read-only banner at the top of the app means the bill is waiting. Open **My plan** to pay it.

## Letting Magnus support help you

Sometimes support needs to see your records to fix a problem. They can only do that if you allow it.

1. Open **Support access** in the menu.
2. Type what you need help with.
3. Tap **Give access for 24 hours**.

### What support can and can't do

- They can only **look**. They can't change anything.
- Passwords, PINs, bank account numbers and TRNs are never shown to them.
- Every screen they open is listed on your Support access page, with who looked and when.
- Access ends by itself after 24 hours, or when you tap **End now**.

Without your permission, Magnus support only sees totals (for example, how many projects you have), never your documents, payroll or clients.

## Ideas and problems

We build Magnus for Caribbean contractors, so we want to hear from you.

1. Tap **Ideas & feedback** in the menu.
2. Choose **An idea**, **Something's not working**, **Something I like** or **Other**.
3. Write what happened or what you would like, and send it.

A problem report goes straight to Magnus support as an alert. If you leave a phone number or email, we can get back to you.

💡 For something urgent, WhatsApp Magnus support. The number is on the website.

## Other things to know

- **System status**: app.magnusboys.com/status shows whether everything is working.
- **Messages from Magnus**: a banner at the top of the app shows news or planned work.
- **Maintenance**: during planned work you may see a "back soon" screen for a short time.
- **Closing your account**: ask Magnus support. You can ask for a copy of your data first. A closed account is deleted after 90 days.
- **Privacy policy and terms**: on the website footer.
